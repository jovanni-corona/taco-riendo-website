About Us

Our Story

Say our name quickly "Taco Riendo" and it sounds like corriendo, "running", which explains our mascot. We're a family-owned taquería on W Inyo Ave in Tulare, serving tacos, burritos, tortas, and much more to all our neighbors. Beyond the restaurant, we cater for parties and other special events. Our goal is good food at a good price, a place where everyone feels welcome.

What Makes Us Special

  • Prices: Tacos are just $2.99
  • Hours: Open 7 days a week, 8 AM to 9 PM
  • Breakfast: Delicious breakfast burritos for the early bird
  • Catering: For parties and events
  • Seating: Outdoor seating, and good for families

Our Values

  • Family
  • Community
  • Welcoming of everyone
